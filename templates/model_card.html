Intended Use

Primary Use Cases

  • Short to medium-term forecasting based on current conditions
  • Decision support for planning and resource allocation
  • Trend analysis and pattern recognition

Out-of-Scope Uses

  • Long-term predictions beyond the model's training horizon
  • Critical decision-making without human oversight
  • Domains with significantly different data distributions

Limitations

  • Model performance may degrade with inputs outside the training distribution
  • Predictions assume similar conditions to historical training data
  • Extreme or unprecedented events may not be accurately predicted
  • Model requires periodic retraining to maintain accuracy

Ethical Considerations

  • Predictions should be used as decision support, not as sole decision-makers
  • Users should understand uncertainty estimates and confidence intervals
  • Model biases should be monitored and addressed through regular audits
  • Transparent communication of model limitations to end users

Model Monitoring

The model's performance is continuously monitored through:

  • Regular validation against new data
  • Tracking of prediction accuracy over time
  • Detection of concept drift and data distribution shifts
  • User feedback collection and analysis
